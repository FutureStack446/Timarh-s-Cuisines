The Journey

Fatima Ibrahim began her culinary journey in 2019 with a simple vision: to bring professional catering standards to the world of luxury home-style baking. What started as a small kitchen operation has blossomed into a premium brand recognized for its attention to detail and uncompromising quality.

Today, Timarh’s Cuisines serves a diverse clientele of food enthusiasts and event planners who seek nothing but the best for their special moments.

Our Mission

To blend traditional catering expertise with contemporary pastry artistry. We aim to create unforgettable culinary experiences that inspire joy and connection through every bite.
